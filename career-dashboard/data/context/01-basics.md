### What this means for the job hunter

Annie **is authorized to work in the US right now**. A posting that says
"must be authorized to work in the United States" does **not** disqualify her — she is.

The ladder:

1. **Now — OPT.** 12 months of post-completion OPT. No employer sponsorship needed. She can start
   immediately at any employer.
2. **Next — STEM OPT extension.** +24 months (36 total) because Computer Engineering is a STEM
   degree. Requires the **employer to be enrolled in E-Verify**. Still no sponsorship needed.
3. **Then — H-1B.** Cap-subject employers enter the annual lottery (~25% selection in FY2026:
   roughly 339,000 registrations for 85,000 slots). **Cap-exempt employers** — universities,
   nonprofit research organizations, government research bodies, many academic medical centers —
   file any time of year with **no lottery at all**.
4. **Possible alternative — O-1.** Depends entirely on whether the publications in
   `06-achievements.md` are real. See ⚠️ Q3 in `QUESTIONS-FOR-YOU.md`.

### The exclusion rule (set by Annie, Sept 2026)

- Posting **explicitly says it will not sponsor** → **excluded**, never shown.
- Posting **says nothing** about sponsorship → **shown**. This is most postings, and many of these
  employers do sponsor once a candidate has cleared the interviews.
- Posting **explicitly offers sponsorship** → shown, ranked top.
- Posting requires **US citizenship, security clearance, ITAR/EAR, or permanent residency** →
  **excluded**. This is a different reason: those roles cannot lawfully employ her regardless of
  how the interview goes.

Absence of H-1B history is **not** a refusal and never removes a company from the list.
