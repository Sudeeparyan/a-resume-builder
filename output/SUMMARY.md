## How the sponsorship rule works

| The posting says | What happens |
|---|---|
| Explicitly **won't sponsor** | Excluded. You never see it. |
| **Says nothing** | **Shown.** Most postings, and where most offers come from. |
| Explicitly **will sponsor** | Shown, ranked top. |
| Needs citizenship / clearance / ITAR | Excluded — they can't hire you regardless. |

Ranking: **S** cap-exempt (no H-1B lottery) → **A** says yes → **B** proven sponsor → **C** silent,
no record.

A company having no H-1B history is **not** a reason to skip it. Most employers never appear in
that data, and plenty sponsor once you've cleared the interviews.

**Sponsor data:** 83,624 employers, USCIS FY2021–2023, refreshed 2026-09-04.
