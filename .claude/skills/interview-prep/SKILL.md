# Interview Prep

## Read first
0. **`context/`** — the real facts behind every story, and `08-voice.md` for how this person actually
   talks. An answer rehearsed in someone else's voice falls apart under follow-up questions.
1. `system/profile/interview-stories.md` — the STAR+R bank
2. `system/profile/master-profile.md` — the facts behind every story
3. `system/profile/positioning.md` — honest gaps and their framing
4. `output/NN_Company_Role/research.md` if deep research was run, plus the JD in `output/`
5. The resume actually sent: `output/NN_Company_Role/resume.tex` — you will be asked about what is on it

## Output

### 1. Interview map
| Likely question | Story to use | Opening line | Risk |
|-----------------|--------------|--------------|------|
Ten to fifteen rows: role-specific technical questions, the standard behavioural set, and the two
or three questions this JD makes inevitable. "Risk" flags where the honest answer is thin.

### 2. Resume defence
Every line on the sent resume is fair game. For each project and role, one paragraph the person can
say out loud: what it did, their specific contribution, the hardest part, what they would change.
Flag anything a sharp interviewer will probe — a reframed project title, a metric with no method
behind it, a skill listed at `exposure` level.

### 3. Gap answers
For each gap in the match assessment, a three-part answer: acknowledge without apologising, name the
closest real experience, state what they are doing about it. No bluffing — an interviewer who
catches an inflated claim stops believing the rest.

### 4. Technical drilling
The 5–8 concepts this JD implies, with a one-paragraph explanation each at the depth an interviewer
would expect for the stated seniority. Note which ones the profile supports at `core` level (safe to
go deep) and which at `exposure` (answer honestly and briefly).

### 5. Their questions
Six questions to ask, drawn from real research: team shape, how success is measured at 6 months,
what the last person in the role found hard, current technical pressures, how decisions get made,
what happens next in the process.

### 6. Logistics
Format, likely duration, panel, what to have open, timezone, and — for remote interviews — a note
to test audio.

## Rules
- Every story must trace to `system/profile/master-profile.md`. If a question has no honest story behind
  it, say so and coach the honest answer instead of manufacturing one.
- Keep the person's own voice from `system/profile/positioning.md → voice notes`. A rehearsed answer in
  someone else's register sounds rehearsed.
- Numbers only where they are real.
