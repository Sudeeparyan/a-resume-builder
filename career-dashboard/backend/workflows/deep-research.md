# Deep Research Workflow

## 1. Confirm the gates

Read `AGENTS.md`, `data/config/profile.yml`, `data/context/evidence.yml` and all of `data/context/`. Stop if the posting refuses sponsorship or requires citizenship, a clearance, ITAR/EAR or permanent residency (it belongs in Excluded roles, with the sentence), if the never-re-apply rules block it, or if it is senior, asks for more than 4 years, sits outside the United States, or is a web/full-stack or DevOps/platform role.

## 2. Verify the posting

Confirm the specific page is active and shows the company, title, US location, description, sponsorship wording and application route.

## 3. Research

Use `backend/workflows/modes/deep.md`: what they do; sponsorship and hiring reality (cap-exempt status, H-1B history, E-Verify); engineering reality; the team and current pressures; where they are going next; what the hire will do; the skills demand map; Annie's evidence-backed angle; questions to ask.

Record source URLs and dates, explicit/inferred status, confidence and the linked requirement ID for every current claim. Prefer the verified JD and first-party employer sources.

Rank every resume-ready project and choose the signature project (unused by other companies in `data/signature-projects.md`, not supporting-only) and one supporting project. Research ranks evidence; it never creates candidate experience or implies work for the target employer.

## 4. Competition and seniority

Posting age and reposts, visible applicant count, years asked versus her dated roles (never state a total), whether the employer accepts research or project evidence, and interview-process evidence from reliable current sources.
