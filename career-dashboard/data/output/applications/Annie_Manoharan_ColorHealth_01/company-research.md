- **First 30 days:** Complete onboarding; learn the relevant service, codebase, development workflow, and clinical/product context; make a small, reviewed change.
- **By 60 days:** Independently deliver a bounded feature or reliability improvement with tests, documentation, and appropriate observability.

## Business, domain, and relevant initiatives

Color describes its current focus as cancer care, with an oncologist-led virtual clinic serving employer, union, health-plan, and public-sector populations. Its care model spans risk identification and screening through treatment and survivorship. ([About Color](https://www.color.com/about-us/), accessed 2026-09-23; [Virtual Cancer Clinic](https://www.color.com/virtual-cancer-clinic/), accessed 2026-09-23). ([color.com](https://www.color.com/about-us/))

Relevant public initiatives include **Cancer Copilot**, which Color says uses OpenAI, large language models, and cancer-guideline knowledge bases to help clinicians identify and order diagnostic workup; and **Color Assistant**, developed with Google Cloud to help assess breast-screening needs and coordinate next steps. These are company initiatives, not evidence that this role will use those vendors or products. ([AI for Cancer Care](https://www.color.com/ai-for-cancer-care/), accessed 2026-09-23). ([color.com](https://www.color.com/ai-for-cancer-care/))

Color reported in a January 2026 post that clinicians use its AI copilot to analyze patient information and prepare diagnostic workups. In May 2026, it announced ASCO certification for its Virtual Cancer Clinic. Both developments make workflow quality, safety, and dependable patient-facing experiences salient domain context; they do not establish this new hire’s team or project. ([January 2026 AI copilot post](https://www.color.com/blog/using-ai-to-accelerate-cancer-diagnosis-to-treatment-time-for-your-workforce/), accessed 2026-09-23; [May 2026 certification announcement](https://www.color.com/blog/first-asco-certified-virtual-oncology/), accessed 2026-09-23).
