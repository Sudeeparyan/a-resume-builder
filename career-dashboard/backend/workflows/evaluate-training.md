# Evaluate Training or Certification

## 1. Read the profile

Read `data/config/profile.yml`, `data/context/05-skills.md` (including the honest gaps) and `data/output/SUMMARY.md` → "What to study this week". The training must support one of her four tracks: Data / Analytics Engineering, ML / AI Engineering, Software Engineering, Embedded / Test Automation.

## 2. Verify current facts

From the provider's official page: exact name, active or retired, current syllabus, prerequisites, exam format, price, renewal policy, and whether it produces a demonstrable project. Never reuse old prices or retired exam codes.

## 3. Score

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Target-track alignment | 25% |
| Hiring signal in current US postings | 20% |
| Closes a gap that recurs across her open applications | 20% |
| Portfolio deliverable | 15% |
| Time and cost | 10% |
| Provider currency and credibility | 10% |

## 4. Compare with alternatives

Applying now; a focused weekend build (a proof artifact from a study plan); deepening Kafka/Flink, AWS or PyTorch evidence she already has; a different current certification.

## 5. Verdict

`DO`, `TIMEBOX` or `SKIP`, with official links, expected benefit, time/cost trade-off and one better alternative when there is one.

A certification she is working toward is never on a resume. Once it is earned, record it in `data/context/06-achievements.md` and the registry; then it can appear.
