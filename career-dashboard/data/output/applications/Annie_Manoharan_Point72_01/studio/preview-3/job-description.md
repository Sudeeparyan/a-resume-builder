What you’ll do

- Design, build, and extend core C# analytics services, APIs, and libraries used across the Macro business.

- Develop and operate high-performance distributed systems with strong guarantees around correctness, latency, and scalability.

- Build real-time and historical analytics services supporting pricing, risk, and global market data workflows.

- Design and maintain time-aware (historical and real-time) data models and services, ensuring consistency across analytics use cases.

- Develop and integrate derived data pipelines and datasets used in research, backtesting, and production analytics.

- Contribute to API design, data modeling, and system architecture for shared analytics platforms.

- Integrate analytics services with batch and streaming data pipelines across research and trading workflows.

- Partner closely with quants and portfolio managers to translate investment requirements into robust platform capabilities.

- Contribute to system reliability through testing, monitoring, and operational best practices.

- Participate in code reviews and technical discussions to improve design, performance, and maintainability.

What’s REQUIRED

- 3–10 years of experience developing production-quality backend software in C#, with a strong focus on distributed systems and APIs (REST/gRPC).

- Proven experience designing, deploying, and supporting backend services or platforms in cloud or hybrid environments .

- Strong understanding of distributed system design, including data modeling, consistency, and time-series or event-driven architectures.

- Hands-on experience with streaming and messaging systems, such as Kafka, and building scalable data pipelines with derived data generation and persistence.

- Familiarity with containerized environments, such as Kubernetes, and modern data and storage technologies.

- Solid grasp of SDLC best practices, including testing, code coverage, monitoring, and operational support in production systems.

- Experience with Python and exposure to C++, particularly in performance- or analytics-oriented contexts.

- Awareness of financial markets and instruments, including rates, FX, and derivatives, and how pricing and risk analytics are applied.

- Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work closely with investment professionals.

- Commitment to the highest ethical standards.
