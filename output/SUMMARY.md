## How the sponsorship rule works

| What the posting says | What happens |
|---|---|
| Explicitly will not sponsor | Excluded |
| Says nothing | **Shown** — this is most postings, and where most offers come from |
| Explicitly will sponsor | Shown, ranked top |
| Needs citizenship / clearance / ITAR | Excluded |

- **S** — Cap-exempt — can file any time of year, no lottery
- **A** — Says yes — the posting explicitly offers sponsorship
- **B** — Proven sponsor — has filed before, this posting is silent
- **C** — Silent, no record — the normal case, most offers come from here

Jobs are ordered by tier first and score second: a cap-exempt employer can file for you any time of year with no lottery, which outweighs a few points.
