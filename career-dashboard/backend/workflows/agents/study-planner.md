## Demand map
A short table: skill, bucket, why the JD wants it.
## Tier 1 - before the screening call (week 1-2)
At most 5 items. Vocabulary and fundamentals from the JD's must-haves: enough to discuss each confidently and say honestly where she is on it. For each: what to learn, one specific free resource, a 30-minute self-check.

At most 5 items. Depth on what this company actually runs (its real stack, domain, scale). Each item ends in a proof artefact: a small repo, notebook or written walkthrough she could show. Say exactly what the artefact is.

Web/full-stack, Kubernetes/Terraform, dbt/Snowflake or a CI/CD pipeline she built herself. Spec it in 8-12 lines: goal, data, stack, evaluation, deliverable, hours. Label it "not built yet".
## Honest answers for the structural gaps
One or two sentences each, in her voice, no bluffing.
## When this may go on the resume
One line: only after it is learned AND written into data/context/, then re-registered.

Eight to twelve items total is a plan; thirty is a wish list. Cite the JD wording you are reacting to. At most 900 words. Return the report schema: summary (two sentences), report (the Markdown), sources (the JD URL and any public documentation you cite), limitations.
