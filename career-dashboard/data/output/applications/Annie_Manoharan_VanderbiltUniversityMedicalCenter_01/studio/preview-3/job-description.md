Job Summary:
The Data Platform Services team is looking for a Data Engineer / Platform Administrator with an inquisitive, analytical mindset who wants to deepen their technical skills. About 80% of your time will go to administering our Azure Databricks platform of 100+ workspaces: building automation, monitoring performance and costs, managing configuration, testing new features, and promoting best practices. The remaining 20% will be spent as a data engineer, building and optimizing data pipelines alongside the rest of the team.

You’ll own defined areas of the platform from the start, with support from senior engineers and room to take on broader scope as you grow.

Note: This role can be performed fully remote from most US locations
.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Platform Administration ( ~80% )

- Administer our Azure Databricks environment of 100+ workspaces, including compute policies, access controls, and governance, working with senior team members on larger architectural decisions

- Build automation and Infrastructure as Code to provision and manage workspaces at scale

- Build solutions for proactive monitoring of platform health, performance, and costs

- Test new Databricks features and promote best practices to teams across the organization

- Develop tools and services that other teams use to create, test, and deliver data-related assets

Data Engineering ( ~20% )

- Design and develop performance-focused data pipelines that support a variety of source systems (flat files, APIs, databases, and more)

- Troubleshoot issues with production pipelines and work with internal stakeholders to deploy fixes

- Build reusable solutions for recurring problems and help ensure data accuracy and integrity

- Work with stakeholders to identify data needs and look for opportunities to improve platform scalability
